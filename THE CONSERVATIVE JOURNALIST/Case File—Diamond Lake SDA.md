## Overview

**Diamond Lake SDA** is a Seventh-day Adventist Church located in Diamond Lake, Washington. It is located at **326002 Highway 2, Newport, WA 99156, United States**. Quite a small church, the members are mostly in their *60s* and *70s*. 
## Members
### Lloyd, Russell

Russell Lloyd (known as Russ) is an older man in his late seventies or early eighties. He is well established and acquainted with the Diamond Lake Seventh-day Adventist Church, as he and his wife Peggy has attended this church since 2020. ### Lloyd, Peggy
