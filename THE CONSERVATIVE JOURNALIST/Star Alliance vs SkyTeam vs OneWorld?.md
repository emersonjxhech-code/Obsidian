## Major Alliance Overview and Current Scale (2026)
1. **Star Alliance**—26 member airlines, 190 countries, 1,300+ airports, 19,000+ daily flights, 762 million customers. *Key Players:* United Airlines (US), Lufthansa (Europe), Singapore Airlines, ANA, Air India, Turkish Airlines (Asia). Largest airline alliance and strongest global footprint, covering 90% of the world and approximately **23%** of the global scheduled traffic market share.
2. **SkyTeam**—18 active full member airlines, with Aeroflot (Russia) currently terminated. 145 countries, 13,800 daily flights, 945 destinations, 605 million customers. *Key Players:* Delta Air Lines (Americas), Air France, KLM, SAS (Scandinavian Airlines), Virgin Atlantic (Europe), Korean Air (Asia). The alliance covers approximately 85% of the world's population and 13.7% of the global market share. It is the second largest airline alliance in the world.
