Horns have a bony core surrounded by a hollow sheath of hardened skin called horn. Horns are usually found on both males and females, while antlers are found only on adult male deer. ###### Thought Provoker
1. The platypus is often considered undeveloped by evolutionists because of its differences as a mammal. However, its unique bill displays contrarily. While it hunts underwater, the platypus closes its eyes, ears, and nostrils. A quick hunter, the animal can only stay submerged for periods of 1-2 minutes. When the bill is placed in water, thousands of tiny holes in a platypus's beak open. Muscular contractions created by the platypus's prey generate electricity which triggers about 40,000 of the receptors on a platypus's beak. These receptors are arranged in such an order as the allow the platypus to determine the precise and exact location of the prey. Another 60,000 other receptors are used to respond to touch. These receptors detect water currents stimulated by the prey, which enables the platypus to determine the distance to its prey. Working together, the electrical and touch receptors allow the platypus to use its bill to "see" underwater. This very advanced technique of fishing shows God's magnificent creative power.
