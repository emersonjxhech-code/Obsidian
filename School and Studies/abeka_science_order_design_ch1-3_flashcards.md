# Abeka Science Order and Design Ch 1-3 Flashcards

Use these for spaced repetition. Format: **Front** | **Back**

## Chapter 1 Flashcards
**What is the study of body structure?** | Anatomy  
**What does fauna describe?** | Animals  
**How many independent variables in a good experiment?** | One  
**What is a sensible explanation to a scientific problem?** | Hypothesis  
**Sum of all chemical processes of life?** | Metabolism  
**Stable internal conditions?** | Homeostasis  
**Scientist who developed scientific method?** | Sir Francis Bacon  
**Science of classification?** | Taxonomy  
**Distinct type of organism?** | Species  
**Living things?** | Organisms  

## Chapter 2 Flashcards
**Process by which plants make food?** | Photosynthesis  
**Early growth of a seed?** | Germination  
**Uniting of sperm and egg?** | Fertilization  
**Ripened flower ovary?** | Fruit  
**Plant tissues that carry food downward?** | Phloem  
**Family that restores nitrogen to soil?** | Pea family  
**Flowering plants?** | Angiosperms  
**Water enters root hairs by?** | Osmosis  
**Young plant no longer dependent on cotyledons?** | Seedling  
**Creeping stem on soil surface?** | Stolon  

## Chapter 3 Flashcards
**Air sacs in lungs?** | Alveoli  
**Windpipe?** | Trachea  
**Dome-shaped muscle aiding breathing?** | Diaphragm  
**Liquid part of blood?** | Plasma  
**Tiny vessels for exchange?** | Capillaries  
**Largest lymphatic organ?** | Spleen  
**Master gland?** | Pituitary gland  
**Primary skin pigment?** | Melanin  
**Upper chambers of heart?** | Atria  
**Connects muscles to bones?** | Tendons  
**Controls simple reflexes?** | Spinal cord  
**Type of joint in shoulder/hip?** | Ball-and-socket  
**Tough membrane around heart?** | Pericardium
