## Down to Egypt: Chapter 3

###### 3.1.
Ham's son is **Mizraim**. 
	-The Greek historian **Herodotus**. 
	-He marvaled at the huge tombs called **pyramids** and the mysterious **sphinxes** (`large stone statues with the heads of men, rams, or hawks and the bodies of lions`. ) He also saw the **hieroglyphics** (`sacred carvings`), the strange writings of the Egyptians. 
	-**Joseph** became the chief assistant to an Egyptian king when he interpreted the king's dreams.
	-**The Rosetta Stone** was uncovered in A.D. 1799. The French scholar **Jean Francis Champollion** worked for fourteen years until he had solved the riddle of the Rosetta Stone.
###### 3.2
The Nile River is the longest in the world.
	-The Nile Delta is *one of the most fertile areas in the world*. 
	-From the **papyrus** plants that grew along the river banks, the Egyptians made boats, baskets, boxes, mats, sandals, and furniture, as well as paper.
	-Rapids called cataracts in the south prevented the Nile from being used to invade Egypt.
###### 3.3
Egyptian kings are called **Pharaohs**. 
	The Eygptians' belief in an afterlife also led them to preserve bodies throught a process known as **mummification**. 
###### 3.4
Early Egypt was divided into two main parts: **Lower Egypt and Upper Egypt**. 
	-Both parts were divided into what the Greeks later called **nomes**. 
	-**Menes** united the two kingdoms and became *the first pharaoh of all Egypt*. He haed the city of **Memphis** built as his capital near where the Lower and Upper Egypt met. 
	-Beginning with Menes, Eghypt was ruled by a sweries of **dynasties** (`families within which the right to be passed from one member to another`). 
	-Egyptian history is divided into three parts: the **Old Kingdom,** the **Middle Kingdom**, and the **New Kingdom.** 
	-Egypt ceased to be a major world power after about 1000 B.C. 
	-The earliest tombs the pharaohs had built for themselves were simple brick structures called **mastabas** with flat tops and sloping sides.
	-The three most famous pyriamids were build in the area of **Giza**. The first and largest pyramid, called the **Great Pyramid,** was built for the pharaoh **Khufu** (`also known as Cheops`). 
	-The second largest pyramid at Giza was built for the pharaoh **Khafre**. The **Great Sphix,** a gigantic statue carved in limestone, with a 13-foor-wide human face in the likeness of a pharaoh—possibly Khafre.
	-**Mortuary temples**
	-**Hatshepsut**, a female pharaoh.
	-**Tribute:** a payment from one nation to another. 
	-Under **Thutmose III**, *the Egyptian Empire reached its greatest extent*—to the Euphrates River in the north and deep into Africa in the south. This territory was held for 150 years, making it the strongest power in the world.
	-Pharaoh's chief assistant: vizier.
	-Ruling class: *nobles and priests*
	-Middle Class: *craftsmen, scribes, and soldiers*
	-Egypt was one huge beauracracy.
	-Lower Class: Farmers and Slaves
