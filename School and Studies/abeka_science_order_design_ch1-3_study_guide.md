## Chapter 1: Invitation to Science (Scientific Method, Characteristics of Life, Classification)

### Key Concepts
- **Purpose of Science**: Carefully observe nature to discover natural laws and apply them for human benefit. Science glorifies God by revealing His design.
- **Scientific Method**: Observation → Hypothesis → Experimentation (hypothesizing, observing, experimenting). Sir Francis Bacon introduced key aspects.
- **Characteristics of Living Things**: Growth, reproduction, metabolism, homeostasis, response to stimuli, organization (cells).
- **Taxonomy**: Science of classification. Hierarchical: Domain, Kingdom, Phylum, Class, Order, Family, Genus, Species. Binomial nomenclature (Genus species).
- **Branches of Biology**: Botany (plants), Zoology (animals), etc.
- **Models**: Partial representations (e.g., photos, diagrams).
- **Variables**: Independent (one per experiment), dependent, controlled.
- **Reasoning**: Inductive (specific to general), deductive.

### Vocabulary
- Anatomy: Study of body structure
- Fauna: Animals
- Flora: Plants
- Homeostasis: Stable internal conditions
- Metabolism: Sum of chemical processes
- Organism: Living thing
- Species: Distinct type of organism
- Hypothesis: Sensible explanation
- Law: Scientific idea surviving many tests
- Inference: Conclusion from evidence

**Practice Questions**:
1. How many independent variables in a good experiment? **One**
2. What is the study of body structure? **Anatomy**

## Chapter 2: Plants: Provision for Life

### Key Concepts
- Plants are foundational for life (photosynthesis, oxygen, food).
- **Flower Structure**: Sepals, petals, stamens (male), pistil (female: stigma, style, ovary).
- **Pollination and Fertilization**: Crucial for reproduction. Pollination transfers pollen; fertilization unites sperm and egg.
- **Seed Dispersal**: Wind, water, animals, etc.
- **Photosynthesis**: Plants make food using light, CO2, water → glucose + oxygen. Chlorophyll in chloroplasts.
- **Plant Tissues**: Xylem (upward water), Phloem (downward food).
- **Leaf Types**: Simple, compound. Venation patterns.
- **Seed Germination**: Radicle (root), etc. Seedling stage.
- Plant Families: Pea (nitrogen-fixing), Grass (grains), Composite.

### Vocabulary
- Photosynthesis: Food manufacturing process
- Germination: Early seed growth
- Fertilization: Sperm + egg
- Fruit: Ripened ovary
- Stolon: Creeping stem
- Angiosperms: Flowering plants
- Monocot/Dicot: Classification based on cotyledons, veins, etc.
- Osmosis: Water movement into roots

**Practice Questions**:
1. What process do plants use to make food? **Photosynthesis**
2. Which family helps restore nitrogen? **Pea family**

## Chapter 3: Your Wonderful Body (Human Anatomy & Physiology)

### Key Concepts
- **Body Systems**: Integumentary (skin), Skeletal, Muscular, Nervous, Endocrine, Cardiovascular, Respiratory, Digestive, Excretory, Immune/Lymphatic, Reproductive.
- **Skeletal System**: Axial (skull, spine, ribcage), Appendicular. Bones, joints (ball-and-socket, etc.), marrow.
- **Muscular System**: Voluntary (skeletal), Involuntary (smooth, cardiac). Tendons, ligaments.
- **Respiratory**: Nose, pharynx, larynx, trachea, bronchi, alveoli, lungs, diaphragm.
- **Circulatory**: Heart (atria, ventricles), blood vessels (arteries, capillaries, veins), blood cells (RBCs, WBCs), plasma.
- **Nervous**: Brain, spinal cord, nerves, impulses.
- **Other**: Homeostasis, tissues (muscle, nerve, etc.), cells.

### Vocabulary
- Alveoli: Air sacs in lungs
- Trachea: Windpipe
- Diaphragm: Breathing muscle
- Plasma: Liquid part of blood
- Capillaries: Tiny vessels
- Lymph nodes, Spleen: Immune
- Pituitary: Master gland
- Melanin: Skin pigment
- Pericardium: Heart membrane

**Practice Questions**:
1. Organ with alveoli? **Lung**
2. Largest lymphatic organ? **Spleen**
3. Dome-shaped muscle for breathing? **Diaphragm**
